# Privacy Policy for YouTube Audio Spatial Splitter

**Last Updated:** December 28, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how "YouTube Audio Spatial Splitter" (the "Extension") handles your data.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
The Extension operates entirely locally within your browser. 
**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), browsing history, or user data to external servers.**

## 2. Permissions
The Extension requires the following permissions to function:
* **Tabs & Host Permissions:** Used solely to detect YouTube tabs and apply audio effects to the video elements on those pages.
* **Storage:** Used to save your audio preferences (e.g., sound position, mode) locally on your device using `chrome.storage.local`. This data never leaves your browser.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The Extension does not use any third-party analytics tools or tracking services.

## 4. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support link on the Chrome Web Store.
